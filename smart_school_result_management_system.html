Privacy & data management

This prototype stores data in browser local storage. Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to access it. Do not use it for real sensitive student data on shared devices until proper authentication, server-side authorization, encrypted backups and access controls are implemented.

Free deployment path

Use this HTML prototype free on Android by opening it in a browser or hosting it on a free static-hosting tier. Static hosting alone does not provide secure multi-user accounts or a shared database; those require a backend and must be configured separately.
